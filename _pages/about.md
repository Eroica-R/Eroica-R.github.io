**Supervisor:** Prof. Xiaolin Hu, Tsinghua Laboratory of Brain and Intelligence, Tsinghua University  
Jul 2024 – Present

- Aimed to provide a convincing simulation of the circadian neural circuits in *Drosophila melanogaster*, uncovering the dynamics and functions of key neurons and synchronization mechanisms within the circuits.

- Conducted an extensive literature review on computational models of neural circuits, particularly focusing on the simulation of circadian rhythms in *Drosophila melanogaster*.

- Gained a comprehensive understanding of the Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF) model and its applications in simulating neural dynamics.

- Mapped distinct daily patterns of fast calcium and slow calcium activity in different cells to conductance and voltage thresholds, simulating circadian neural circuits based on the *Drosophila* connectome and the Brian2 framework.

- Reviewed advancements in modeling Poisson spike trains and the role of V1–V4 brain structures in visual processing, including potential parallels in insect models.
